Slide &Seal Slider Bags

Slide Seal™ is our very own brand of Zipper-Slider bags which is a range of multipurpose storage bags made with high quality food grade recyclable plastic.

The Slide&Seal™ range gives the users a versatile bag which can be used as a sandwich bag, dry fruit storage ,travel bag ,freezer bag among its other uses.

A set of 20 zipper-slider bags are packed in an easy to use carton which conveniently stores the bags of which 3 different sizes are available . The Small(16cmX16 cm) , Medium(20cmX19cm) and Large(26cmX24 cm) bags, each have their own uses and their convenient sliders help the users with an easy access to the contents inside.

  • Small
  • Medium
  • Large

Secure Seal bags
