Company Overview

Leading the Packaging Industry Since 1992

We are manufacturers of Zipper Pouch & Slider Bags, Shrink Film, HM, LD, PP, Tubes & Printed bags, Bio-Degradable Bags, Multilayer Specialty Poly Films, having 4 colors printing.

Founded in 1992 as a Proprietorship by Mr. J.L. Tandon, later on joined by Sumit Tandon and Alka Tandon, converted to a Partnership Firm. We are an organization which is catering to a fairly large and diverse range of customers.
