Vapour Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) Packaging

We manufacture VCI packaging for items prone to corrosion, rusting and oxidation for various packaging requirements. The corrosion-inhibiting compound migrates from our VCI packaging into the packaging environment, adhering to metal surfaces and reaching into recessed areas.

Our VCI Range Includes: VCI Poly Pouches, Shrink Films, Tube/Sheet Roll, 3D Bags, Pallet covers, VCI Paper, RP Oil.
