About Plasto Films

We At Plasto Films are Manufacturers of Zipper Pouches - Slider Bags, Shrink Films, Certified - Biodegradable Packaging materials, Multilayer Poly films, HM-HDPE, LDPE, Polypropene Tubes, Films & Bags, Anti-Microbial, Anti Rust VCI Bags, Industrial packaging material and all kinds of other flexible packaging materials with Multicolour printing facility inhouse. Founded in 1992, having 45 years + of experience in the packaging industry, we can cater to all your Flexible Poly Packaging needs.

Our prime moto is – QUALITY.

We are committed to quality and service to our customers.We use raw material from world leading polymer manufacturers with high quality and high precision.
