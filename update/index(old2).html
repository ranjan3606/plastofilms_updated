About Plasto Films

We are Manufacturers of :Zipper Slider Bags , Shrink Films, 100% Biodegradable Packaging materials and all kinds of other flexible packaging materials. Founded in 1992 as a Proprietorship by Mr. J.L. Tandon later on joined by Sumit Tandon and Alka Tandon Converted to a Partnership Firm, an organization which is catering fairly large and diverse range of customers.

Our prime moto is – QUALITY.

We are committed to quality and service to our customers. The raw materials used are reputed Indian or Overseas manufacturers which go through proper quality control measures.
