About Plasto Films

We are manufacturers of Zipper Pouch & Slider Bags, Shrink Film, HM, LD, PP, Tubes & Printed bags, Bio-Degradable Bags, Multilayer Specialty Poly Films, having 4 colors printing. Founded in 1992 as a Proprietorship by Mr. J.L. Tandon later on joined by Sumit Tandon and Alka Tandon Converted to a Partnership Firm, An organization which is catering fairly large and diverse range of customers.

Our prime moto is – QUALITY.

We are committed to quality and service to our customers. The raw materials used are reputed Indian or Overseas manufacturers which go through proper quality control measures.

Our whole manufacturing processing is carried out under the supervision of trained experts to ensure customers satisfaction.
