The Power Behind Our Products

At Plasto Films, our manufacturing infrastructure is the backbone of our commitment to excellence. Housed in the Okhla Industrial Area, New Delhi, our facilities are equipped with the latest technology in polymer processing. With over three decades of experience, we have continuously upgraded our machinery to ensure we meet and exceed global packaging standards.

Core Production

Advanced Extrusion Technology

Equipped with cutting-edge multilayer extrusion plants, we produce films with superior strength, barrier properties, and unparalleled consistency. Our high-capacity extrusion lines allow us to manufacture custom blends tailored to highly specialized packaging requirements across diverse industries, from industrial heavy-duty films to food-grade materials.

Aesthetics & Branding

State-of-the-Art Printing Facilities

We understand that packaging is the face of your brand. Our premium in-house multi-color printing machinery ensures that every bag and film we produce carries vibrant, accurate, and durable graphics. We offer comprehensive custom printing solutions with exact sizing, vivid color matching, and bespoke designs that make your product stand out on the shelf.

Precision Finishing

Integrated Pouching & Cutting Units

Efficiency meets accuracy in our advanced cutting, pouching, and slitting department. From robust zipper slider bags to customized industrial poly bags, our automated units ensure flawless finishing and robust seals. This integrated approach allows us to maintain strict quality control while offering rapid turnaround times for bulk orders.

Excellence Guaranteed

Stringent Quality Control Lab

Before any product leaves our facility, it undergoes rigorous testing in our dedicated Quality Control Laboratory. As an ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 22000:2018 certified company, our expert technicians test for tensile strength, elongation, seal integrity, and dimensional accuracy to guarantee absolute perfection and compliance with international standards.
